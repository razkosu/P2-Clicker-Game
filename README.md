## Artistic Statement

### Targeted Experience
> *In 1-2 sentences, briefly describe the overall feeling, mood, and core goal you want the player to experience while playing your clicker game.*
I want the player to feel the unfairness of the game and try their best to get as much money as they can while gambling. 

### Design Choices
> *Describe your visual style, audio, and UI choices and how they work in coherence to support your theme.*
My main goal was to create a contrasting foreground and background with the gambling themes combined with nature.
Imagine the feeling of playing slots in the middle of a grass field - that is what I'm going for.

### Game Assets & Placeholders
> *List all art, music, or UI assets that you used to reinforce your theme, and note any placeholders if you cannot find one that fits.*
I used the money tongue emoji for the clicker, slots machine for auto generator, and scenic nature for the background.
I would add more animations for the slots and descriptions of what the chances and consequences are using UI.
